# IdeaMates --- Product Assumptions

# 1. What product are we planning to build?

We are planning to build a platform named **IdeaMates** where students
can create profiles, share their ideas, and also they can find and connect with teammates for projects, startups, hackathons, and other events. The main aim is to help students, turn their ideas into real projects by connecting them with the right people.

# 2. What problem does it solve?

Many students have good ideas but struggle to find teammates to work with. If a student is interested to participate in hackathon or any other activities, this platform helps to find teammates and also the student can discuss with them using this platform. Some may have coding knowledge but lack designing skill, while others have creative ideas but don't know how to develop them. Finding better teammates in social media platform can also be difficult. This platform make these processes easier by connecting them.

# 3. Who are the target users?

The target users are: - College students - School students - Aspiring
entrepreneurs - Startup founders

The platform is especially useful for people who have ideas but need
teammates to help develop them.

# 4. What is the main use case?

Imagine someone wants to create a mobile application but does not have
all the skills needed to build it. They can post their idea on IdeaMates
and mention the type of teammates they are looking for, such as a
developer or designer.

Other people can browse the project and request to join. The project
owner can then choose suitable teammates and start working with them.

# 5. What are the core features?

The main features of IdeaMates include: 
**User registration and login:** Users can create an account and sign in to the platform. 
**User profiles:** Users can add their skills, interests, and
experience. 
**Project posting:** Users can share their ideas and explain what kind of help they need. 
**Search and filters:** Users can find projects and people based on their skills and interests. 
**Join requests:** Users can request to become part of a project. 
**Request management:** Project owners can accept or reject requests. 
**Dashboard:** Users can manage their projects and requests in one
place.

# 6. What assumptions are we making about users?

We assume that many people have ideas they want to develop but struggle
to find the right teammates. We also believe that people are willing to
share their skills and interests to connect with others, and that people
with different skills can work well together when they have a common
goal.

Through IdeaMates, we hope to make it easier for people to find
teammates and bring their ideas to life.

# 7. What will the MVP include?

The first version of IdeaMates will include only the basic features
needed to make the platform useful: - User registration and login -
Profile creation - Project posting - Browsing and searching projects -
Sending join requests - Basic management of projects and join requests

We will focus on these features first and improve the platform based on
user feedback.

# 8. What will the MVP not include?

The first version will not include advanced features such as: - AI-based
teammate matching - Video calls - Real-time chat - Online payments -
Funding services - A mentorship system

These features can be added in the future if users need them. For now,
our main focus is to build a simple platform that helps people find
teammates and collaborate on projects.
